# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, plan testing strategy, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA approach aligned with project milestones
- Define acceptance criteria with Product and Development teams
- Execute and coordinate manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects; track quality metrics
- Validate readiness for release and smoke tests in staging/production

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet customer expectations
- Reduce production incidents and rework
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Daily standups (status on test execution)
- Weekly quality metrics review
- Release coordination and post-deploy verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy, automate test coverage, and triage defects found during development
- **With Product Managers**: Define acceptance criteria and success metrics; validate feature alignment with requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates, flag release readiness issues, and track testing timelines
- **With Technical Leads**: Partner on test architecture and performance validation
- **With Design/UX Leads**: Validate usability and accessibility standards

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads set technical direction, make architectural decisions, mentor developers, and manage technical debt and scalability concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for projects
- Review design proposals and resolve technical trade-offs
- Mentor and support developers on technical challenges
- Identify and prioritize technical debt
- Ensure solutions meet scalability, security, and maintainability standards

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce long-term operational burden and rework
- Build team technical capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and technical mentoring
- Planning sessions (estimation and feasibility)
- Risk identification and mitigation strategy

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Mentor on implementation, review code for architectural alignment, and guide on complex technical challenges
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility, trade-offs between features and technical debt, and scalability requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies; support resource and effort estimation
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Define test architecture and performance benchmarks
- **With Scrum Masters**: Support process improvements and identify process-level technical blockers

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Delivery Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, coach teams on continuous improvement, and ensure team health.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate process blockers and team impediments
- Coach team on agile best practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain project board and process metrics (velocity, cycle time)
- Foster psychological safety and feedback culture

### Goals
- Maintain consistent delivery velocity and predictability
- Remove friction in the delivery process
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives)
- One-on-ones with team members
- Weekly process health check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate on scheduling, escalate blockers, and track process health metrics
- **With All Roles**: Facilitate ceremonies and coach on continuous improvement practices; create psychological safety for feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Support backlog refinement and prioritization ceremonies
- **With Developers**: Support removal of technical and process impediments; coach on team agreements

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, provide budget and decision authority, and ensure alignment with strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve budget, timeline, and scope trade-offs
- Provide executive sponsorship and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate project status and outcomes to leadership
- Ensure alignment with company strategy

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI of projects
- Ensure transparency and stakeholder confidence
- Enable fast decision-making and unblocking

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Milestone sign-offs and go/no-go decisions
- Executive briefings and risk escalations
- Ad-hoc consultation on priority and scope changes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide decisions and approvals on scope, budget, and timeline; escalation path for critical issues
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business strategy and priorities; provide market and business context
- **With Developers and QA**: May attend demos and reviews to validate business outcomes
- **With Technical Leads**: Understand and approve major architectural decisions with business implications
- **With Scrum Masters**: Engaged for retrospectives affecting organizational processes

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience strategy, ensure accessibility and usability, and maintain design consistency across products.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience and design approach for features
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create design specifications, wireframes, and prototypes
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG) and design system consistency
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and polish

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce support burden through clear, usable designs
- Maintain consistent brand and design language

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and design discovery sessions
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Usability testing and user feedback incorporation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Define user experience requirements and validate against user research and product goals
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on implementation details, feasibility, and refinement of designs; provide design specifications
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Partner on usability testing and accessibility validation
- **With Project Managers**: Provide design timelines and manage design dependencies
- **With Technical Leads**: Discuss technical feasibility of design approaches and performance considerations
